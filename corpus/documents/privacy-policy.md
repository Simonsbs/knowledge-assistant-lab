## Workspace deletion

After a workspace deletion request is approved, active workspace data is deleted within 30 days. The workspace owner must confirm the request before deletion begins.

## Backup deletion

Deleted workspace data can remain in encrypted backups for up to 90 days before backup expiry removes it. Backup data is not restored into an active workspace except through an approved recovery process.
