## Maintenance window

Scheduled maintenance takes place on Tuesdays from 02:00 to 03:00 UTC. The status page lists each approved maintenance window and its expected impact.

## Advance notice

Customers receive at least seven days of advance notice for scheduled maintenance. Emergency maintenance can occur without the normal notice period, with updates on the status page.
