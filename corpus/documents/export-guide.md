## Export format

Workspace exports use CSV files encoded as UTF-8. An export includes records that the requesting user is authorised to access in the selected workspace.

## Download expiry

An export download link expires after 48 hours. Request a new export if the download link has expired. Download links cannot be extended.

## Audit history

Only a workspace owner can export the audit history. Open Settings > Audit > Export and choose the required date range. Support staff cannot export a customer's audit history on the customer's behalf.
