## Recovery objectives

The recovery point objective (RPO) is four hours. The recovery time objective (RTO) is eight hours. These are fictional planning objectives, not recovery performance measurements or a contractual uptime guarantee.

## Data location

The primary data location is Dublin, Ireland. Encrypted backups are stored in Frankfurt, Germany. Both locations are within the European Union.
