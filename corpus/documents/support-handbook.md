## Priority one

For a priority one (P1) incident, the initial response target is 15 minutes, available 24 hours a day, seven days a week. A priority one incident means the production service is unavailable for all users. This is a fictional support target, not a measured service result.

## Priority two

For a priority two (P2) incident, the initial response target is four business hours. Business hours are Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 UTC, excluding the fictional company's published holidays.

## Incident escalation

If a priority one incident has no assigned owner after 30 minutes, the support lead must open an incident bridge and page the duty incident commander.

## Customer updates

During an active priority one incident, the incident commander sends a customer status update every 30 minutes. Updates state the known impact, current investigation and time of the next update.
